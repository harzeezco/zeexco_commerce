FEATURE
   │
   ├── VALUE
   │     What problem does this solve?
   │
   ├── ASSUMPTION
   │     What must be true for this to work?
   │
   ├── RISK
   │     How could this hurt merchants,
   │     customers or Zeexco?
   │
   ├── CONTROL
   │     What reduces that risk?
   │
   └── EVIDENCE
         How will we know whether our
         assumptions and controls work?
